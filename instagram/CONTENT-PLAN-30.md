# First 30 Posts — Launch Sequence

Ordered deliberately. The first 9 establish credibility before anything is ever
sold. No affiliate link appears before post 10.

**Cadence:** 5/week (Mon–Fri). ~6 weeks of runway.
**Default format:** carousel, 6–8 slides, 1080×1350 (4:5).
**Slide 1 is 80% of the job.** If the hook slide fails, nothing else matters.

Legend — **B**uild · **BD** Breakdown · **F**ailure · **C**omparison · **T**eardown

## Caption Formula

```
[HOOK — repeats/sharpens slide 1, one line]

[CONTEXT — 2-3 lines, what you did and why it mattered]

[PAYLOAD — the actual value, 3-5 short lines or a mini-list]

[HONESTY LINE — one limitation, cost, or caveat. Never skip this one.]

[CTA — exactly one. Save / DM keyword / link in bio. Never stack them.]

[disclosure if affiliate]
.
.
#3-5 tight niche hashtags — not 30
```

The honesty line is the differentiator. Every competitor omits it, and it is
the reason people believe the rest.

## Production Batch Workflow

Once monthly, ~3 hours:

1. **Pick 20 posts** from the plan → lock hooks first, before any design
2. **Draft all copy** in one sitting — voice stays consistent this way
3. **Generate visuals** in batch from the locked template
4. **Human editorial pass** — mandatory, every post, no exceptions
5. **Queue** via scheduler or Graph API
6. **Leave engagement manual** — this is the part that must never be automated

Reply to every comment for the first 90 days. That is not optional, and it is
the highest-ROI hour you will spend each week.
