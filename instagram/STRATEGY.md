# AI Tools Instagram Page — Strategy

**Owner:** Chirag | **Base:** Melbourne, AU | **Started:** Aug 2026
**Status:** Pre-launch

---

## The Positioning

> **"I'm learning to build with AI in public. Here's what actually worked, and what broke."**

Not a guru account. A builder's logbook.

The niche "AI tools" is drowning in `10 AI TOOLS YOU NEED 🤯` listicle slop, all
recycled from the same sources by people who have never opened the tool. That
saturation is the opportunity, not the problem — the bar for *actually
demonstrating a working build* is on the floor.

### Why this specific angle wins

| Force | How the angle answers it |
|---|---|
| Meta's 2026 authenticity push (Mosseri memo) | Real builds, real failures, real screenshots. Nothing to fake. |
| AI content reach concerns | AI is the *subject*, so AI visuals are on-theme, not a tell. |
| Grifter saturation | Competitors cannot fake a working build. This is the moat. |
| Needs to convert, not just reach | People who watch you build hire you to build. Lead-gen is native. |
| Chirag is job hunting | Every post is portfolio. The page doubles as proof of skill. |

**The triple-purpose test:** every post should serve at least two of —
(1) affiliate revenue, (2) service leads, (3) job-hunt portfolio.
If it serves none, don't post it.

## Revenue Stack

Sequenced. Do not skip ahead.

### Phase 1 — Affiliate (Month 1–3)
Start here because it validates whether the audience *buys* before we build
anything. Only promote tools genuinely used in a real build. One burned
recommendation costs more than one commission earns.

**Shortlist (recurring-commission first):**

| Tool | Commission | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| beehiiv | up to 50% / 12mo, 60-day cookie | Creator-economy fit, tiered |
| Kit (ConvertKit) | 50% / 12mo + lifetime bonus at Bronze | Email tooling, high intent |
| Writesonic | 30% lifetime recurring | Writing |
| Murf AI | ~30% recurring, 60-day cookie | Voice — for the Reels workflow |
| Voibe | 25% recurring, no 12mo cap | Uncapped is rare |
| Semrush | $200–450 flat, 120-day cookie | High-value bounty |
| Webflow | 50% first year, 90-day cookie | Pairs with site builds |

Rule: max 3 active affiliate relationships at once. More than that and the
account reads as a billboard.

### Phase 2 — Lead-gen for service (Month 2+, runs parallel)
**Offer:** AI workflow automation for Melbourne small businesses.
This is the highest revenue-per-follower path by a wide margin, and it needs
*credibility*, not scale — 2,000 right followers beats 50,000 wrong ones.

Ladder: free teardown post → DM conversation → paid audit → build engagement.
Also the strongest job-interview material you can possibly have.

### Phase 3 — Brand deals (Month 6+)
Realistic only past ~10K engaged followers. Inbound will start before you're
ready; hold rates until you have a media kit with real conversion numbers,
not just reach.

## Content Pillars

Five pillars, rotating. Ratios tuned so the page teaches far more than it sells.

| # | Pillar | Share | Format | Serves |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Build** — I made X, here's the workflow | 30% | Carousel | Portfolio + leads |
| 2 | **The Breakdown** — how this tool actually works | 25% | Carousel | Affiliate |
| 3 | **The Failure** — what broke and why | 15% | Carousel / Reel | Trust (highest-saving content) |
| 4 | **The Comparison** — X vs Y on a real task | 20% | Carousel | Affiliate (highest intent) |
| 5 | **The Teardown** — free audit of a real business workflow | 10% | Reel + CTA | Leads |

**Format priority:** carousels lead. 2026 data has carousels outperforming
Reels and static for engagement, and they are dramatically cheaper to produce.
Reels are for reach spikes and the teardown pillar only.

**The Failure pillar is non-negotiable.** It is the single hardest thing for a
competitor to copy, the most-saved format, and it is what makes the affiliate
recommendations believable when they come.

## Non-Negotiables

1. **Never promote an untested tool.** Every affiliate link follows a real build.
2. **Label AI content.** Use the AI Creator label. It is not a ranking penalty
   and getting caught undisclosed is a credibility reset.
3. **No fake persona.** Faceless is fine. Fabricated human is not — disclosure
   rules make it fragile and brand deals evaporate on discovery.
4. **No fabricated results.** No invented revenue screenshots, no fake client
   logos, no borrowed case studies. The entire moat is that this account is true.
5. **Disclose affiliate links.** `#ad` / `affiliate` in caption. ACCC rules
   apply in Australia and the disclosure costs nothing.
6. **Ship ugly over nothing.** Consistency beats polish at this stage.

## Success Metrics

Vanity metrics are a trap. Track these instead, weekly.

**Leading (month 1–2)**
- Saves per post ← *the single best early signal*
- Shares per post
- Profile visits → follow conversion %
- Link-in-bio click-through rate

**Lagging (month 3+)**
- Email list signups per week
- Affiliate clicks → conversions
- Qualified DM conversations per week
- Revenue per 1,000 followers ← the only number that really matters

**Kill criteria:** if after 90 days of consistent posting the account has under
500 followers AND under 5 qualified DMs, the positioning is wrong. Change the
angle, not the effort level.

## Risks, Honestly

| Risk | Reality | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Niche is saturated | It genuinely is | Win on real builds; grifters can't fake them |
| AI reach penalties | Contested; mass-produced low-context output is the flagged behaviour | High-context posts, human editorial pass on everything |
| Affiliate income is slow | First commission realistically month 2–4 | Lead-gen pays faster; don't bank on affiliate alone |
| Time collapse during job hunt | The real risk | Batch monthly. 90 min/week floor keeps it alive |
| Meta API app review | Takes weeks | Start immediately, post manually meanwhile |
| Burnout on a fake-feeling topic | Kills most pages by week 6 | The build-in-public angle means posting = learning anyway |
